# Breaking the link

> The one break-it exercise the capstone keeps. Tap the SPI wire between host and
> Egret, replay a captured signed response, then modify one in flight: a
> man-in-the-middle on the bus the reader just built. Show how attestation
> freshness (a host-chosen nonce or challenge) and response signing defeat replay
> and tamper, and state plainly what the raw channel does not protect on its own.
> This is the demonstrative payoff, and unlike the old five-attack list it lives
> on the integration's own wire, reusing the signing and freshness guarantees the
> Attestation part proved.
